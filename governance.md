# Governance

Nomic has successfully passed its security audit and has removed the restriction that previously halted BTC deposits when the bridge reached a capacity limit of 21 BTC.

A beta governance system is currently in place. A future upgrade will be released which will introduce a novel futarchy-based governance mechanism with a full suite of governance tools.

A proposal for a revenue sharing agreement between Nomic and Osmosis has been approved. Under this agreement, Bitcoin bridging fees will be waived when a transaction originates or terminates on Osmosis. In exchange, a proportion of taker fees generated by trading nBTC or its derivatives on Osmosis will be shared with Nomic. The percentage of nBTC taker fees allocated to the split is currently controlled by Osmosis and Nomic Governance.
