# Governance

During the early stages of the bridge, whilst Nomic underwent auditing, a capacity limit of 21 BTC was implemented to slow the growth of the reserve. When the bridge reached this limit, applications could not generate deposit addresses, and users were unable to deposit more BTC.

Since successfully passing the security audit, a new proposal was passed to remove the capacity limit, enabling greater adoption and usage. A beta governance system remains in place, with voting rights temporarily restricted to validators.

A future upgrade will introduce a novel futarchy-based governance mechanism for lifting the NOM transfer restriction and selecting other chain parameters.
